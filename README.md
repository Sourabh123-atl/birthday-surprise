# A Special Birthday Surprise Website 🎁✨
**A Heartfelt Gift from Sourabh to His Best Friend**

A beautifully crafted, mobile-first birthday celebration website designed like opening a series of five thoughtful gifts.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

### 🎁 Opening Experience
- **Closed Gift Box**: “Someone saved a little birthday surprise just for you 🎁”
- **Button**: “Open your gift”
- **Reveal**: “Happy Birthday, My Best Friend! 🎂💛” with celebratory animation and her featured portrait with her baby.

---

### 🎀 The Five Sequential Gifts

1. **Gift 1 — A note for you 💌**
   - Elegant stationery letter card with the exact heartfelt message from Sourabh.
   - Beautiful deckle edge, drop cap typography, and authentic handwritten sign-off.

2. **Gift 2 — Little moments, big smiles 📸**
   - All **14 distinct photos** cleanly cropped without any phone/Instagram UI or distortion.
   - Mobile touch swipe support, tap-to-enlarge Lightbox modal with next/prev navigation and simple respectful captions.

3. **Gift 3 — Your beautiful chapters 🌷**
   - Three luxury cards celebrating her journey:
     - **“The friend you are”** (celebrating her warmth, positivity & unique spark)
     - **“A new chapter together”** (celebrating married life & home)
     - **“The sweetest little chapter”** (celebrating motherhood & baby sweetness)

4. **Gift 4 — A pocketful of wishes ✨**
   - Five interactive glowing stars:
     - 🕊️ **Peace**
     - 😄 **Laughter**
     - 🌟 **Dreams**
     - 🏡 **Family Moments**
     - 🌸 **Time for Yourself**
   - Interactive flip cards with live unlocked progress counter.

5. **Gift 5 — One final surprise 🎂**
   - Featured portrait birthday card.
   - Closing message: *“If today gives you even half as many smiles as you’ve given the people around you, it will be a wonderful day. Happy Birthday, bestie! 🎈💛”*
   - Interactive **Virtual Birthday Cake** with candle blow-out ceremony, smoke puff, fireworks, and confetti.
   - **“↺ Replay the surprise”** button to reset and re-experience the gift journey.

---

## 🎵 Audio / Music
- Fully optional, **muted initially by default**.
- Toggleable via the visible header button.
- Synthesized in pure Web Audio API (warm celesta/music box chime) without external audio dependencies.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open a terminal in this directory:
   ```bash
   cd C:\Users\patel\.gemini\antigravity-ide\scratch\birthday-celebration-letter
   ```
2. Start any local web server (Python, Node, or VS Code Live Server):
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 8080
   ```
3. Open `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.
